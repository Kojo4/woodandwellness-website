We plan, staff, equip and accredit practical trade blocks for independent and specialist schools, so your learners get real construction skills without you recruiting a tutor for every trade.

Five blocks, one provider

Each trade runs as its own six week block. Book one to start, or build a full year of construction with us. Most schools start with carpentry and add a trade each half term.

Carpentry and joinery

Measuring, marking and cutting joints by hand, then with 3D printed jigs and handheld CNC. Learners leave with a finished piece they designed.

Bricklaying

Setting out, mixing and laying to a line, checked with spirit levels and laser lines. Brick bays can be built on site as a joinery project.

Plumbing

Cutting, bending and joining pipe, compression and push fit fittings, and testing for leaks on practice rigs.

Electrical

Circuits, switches and safe wiring practice on low voltage training boards, with every finished item inspected and tested by a competent adult.

3D printing and digital making

Designing in Tinkercad, printing parts and combining them with timber to make real products, such as lamps and coat stands.

Progress your SENCO can use

An occupational therapist works alongside our tutors. Every learner is assessed at the start and end of the block, so you see measured change, not just attendance.

  1. Before week one

    We read the EHCPs

    The OT reviews each plan with your team and sets practical goals for every learner.

  2. Every session

    We measure in the workshop

    Fine and gross motor skills, motor planning, coordination, sensory regulation, confidence and teamwork.

  3. End of block

    You get a report per learner

    Progress mapped to EHCP outcomes and ready for annual reviews, plus portfolio evidence for accreditation.

What schools ask us

How big are the groups?

Small, so every learner gets time on the tools. We agree numbers with you based on your learners' needs and the space.

Who delivers the sessions?

A specialist tutor for each trade, supported by our occupational therapist. Everyone holds an enhanced DBS check and has child and adult safeguarding training.

Is it safe for learners with complex needs?

Yes. Every tool and task has a written risk assessment, sessions are planned around each EHCP, and electrical work uses low voltage boards. Nothing is plugged in until it has been inspected and tested.

How much does a block cost?

Every school is different, so we quote for your setting, your learners and the trades you choose. Get in touch and we will send a suggested programme within two working days.

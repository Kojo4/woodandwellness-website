Our approach

Cut it by hand. Cut it by machine.

Learners do every task the traditional way, then again with the tools the trade uses today. They time it, measure it and decide which a real workshop should use.

Traditional

  • Saw, chisel and housing joints
  • String line and spirit level
  • Screw terminals and compression joints
  • Plans drawn on paper
VS

Modern

  • Handheld CNC and 3D printed jigs
  • Self levelling laser lines
  • Lever connectors and push fit
  • Tinkercad, thermal and inspection cameras

How a block runs

Six weeks, start to finish

STEP 1

Plan

We review EHCPs with the occupational therapist and set each learner's goals.

STEP 2

Build

Weekly 90 minute sessions with a specialist tutor, all tools and materials supplied.

STEP 3

Visit

A day on a live building site to see the trade at full scale.

STEP 4

Show

A showcase for families and staff, certificates, and a report for every learner.

About us

Making as wellbeing, and as a way into work

Wood and Wellness began with a simple idea: making things with your hands is good for you. Today we run accredited construction and product design programmes across London, from specialist schools to community workshops.

Every block is designed by us, delivered by qualified tutors, and backed by safeguarding, insurance and clear risk assessments, so settings can offer real trade skills without building a department from scratch.

Founded and led by Kojo Sarpong, Director.

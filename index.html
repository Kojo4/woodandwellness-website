Six weeks, start to finish

Every block runs ready to go, with tutors, materials, safety and accreditation handled by us.

  1. Week 0

    Plan

    We review EHCPs with our occupational therapist and set each learner's goals.

  2. Weeks 1 to 5

    Build

    Weekly sessions with a specialist tutor, all tools, materials and PPE supplied.

  3. Site day

    Visit

    A day on a live building site to see the trade at full scale.

  4. Week 6

    Show

    A showcase for families and staff, certificates, and a report for every learner.

Evidence, not just a nice afternoon

An occupational therapist assesses every learner at the start and end of each block. Schools get progress reports mapped to EHCP outcomes, ready for annual reviews.

  • Measured

    Fine motor skills

    Grip, precision and tool control.

  • Measured

    Gross motor skills

    Lifting, posture and coordination.

  • Measured

    Motor planning

    Sequencing a task from start to finish.

  • Measured

    Bilateral coordination

    Using both hands together on a tool.

  • Measured

    Sensory regulation

    Noise, texture and focus in a working space.

  • Measured

    Confidence and teamwork

    Roles, reflection and pride in work.

FAQ

How big are the groups?

Small, so every learner gets time on the tools. We agree numbers with you based on your learners' needs and the space.

Who delivers the sessions?

A specialist tutor for each trade, supported by our occupational therapist. Everyone holds an enhanced DBS check and has child and adult safeguarding training.

Is it safe for learners with complex needs?

Yes. Every tool and task has a written risk assessment, sessions are planned around each EHCP, and electrical work uses low voltage boards.

How much does a block cost?

Every setting is different, so we quote for your learners and the trades you choose. Get in touch and we will send a suggested programme within two working days.

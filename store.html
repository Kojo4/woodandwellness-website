The Cocoon Lamp

£49.90

A lamp about becoming. A silkworm spins almost a kilometre of silk around itself, and quietly, out of sight, everything changes. We sketched that shape again and again, then printed it layer by layer in plant based PLA. Switched on, it glows the way a cocoon does: soft, warm and protective.

From learner to maker

Making is good for wellbeing. It can also be a first step into work. Our pathway takes people from their first session to selling what they make.

  1. Stage 1

    Learn

    Practical sessions with traditional and modern tools.

  2. Stage 2

    Make

    Design and build finished products worth keeping.

  3. Stage 3

    Sell

    Selected pieces go on sale in the Wood and Wellness Store.

  4. Stage 4

    Grow

    Social media marketing and selling skills to build a maker business.

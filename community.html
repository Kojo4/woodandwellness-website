Wood and Wellness CIC

People arrive unsure. They leave having made something.

Our community interest company runs making programmes for young people, youth justice services and adult learners across London, and helps the people who want to keep going turn a skill into work.

Track record

Enfield Council Summer University

For two summers back to back, we delivered making workshops on Enfield Council's Summer University for more than 100 young people aged 11 to 25, including young people with special educational needs. Each year ended with documented outcomes and an impact report for the council.

Youth justice

Focus, skills and a route on

Small group, hands on sessions that give young people something real to show for their time, with accredited units along the way.

  • Small groups, run with your team
  • Accredited units through ASDAN and AQA
  • Where funding allows, an equipment budget so young people can set up as independent makers

Adult and community

Learn a skill, feel the difference

Product design courses for adult learners and community groups, combining woodworking, 3D printing and electronics, with wellbeing at the centre.

  • Beginner friendly, no experience needed
  • Mobile kit for community centres and libraries
  • Every learner leaves with a finished product

From learner to maker

A skill that can become work

Making is good for wellbeing. It can also be a first step into work. Our pathway takes people from their first session to selling what they make.

  1. Learn

    Practical sessions with traditional and modern tools.

  2. Make

    Design and build finished products worth keeping.

  3. Sell

    Selected pieces go on sale in the Wood and Wellness Store.

  4. Grow

    Learn social media marketing and selling to build a maker business.
